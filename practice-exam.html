How the page marks you. Most questions get full marks or none here. In the real exam you also earn marks for correct working, so when an answer is wrong, compare your working with the solution line by line. Two questions (a graph and a “show that”) you mark yourself against the model answer.

Typing algebra. Type x² as x^2, and use brackets: 2x(5x+2). Negative numbers can be typed with an ordinary minus sign.

Your answers are kept on this device as you type, so you can stop and come back.
